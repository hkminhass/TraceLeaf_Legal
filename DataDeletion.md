# TraceLeaf — Data Deletion Request

## Requesting Deletion of Your Data

At TraceLeaf, we respect your privacy and provide users with a way to request deletion of personal data that TraceLeaf directly handles.

To request deletion of your TraceLeaf data, please send an email to:

**traceleafofficial@gmail.com**

### What to Include

Please use the subject line:

**TraceLeaf Data Deletion Request**

In your email, please include enough information for us to identify your request.

If your request relates to Google Drive Backup, you may also include the Google account email address associated with your TraceLeaf backup so that we can better understand your request.

We may ask for additional information to verify the request and protect your data from unauthorized deletion.

---

## What Data Can Be Deleted?

Depending on how you use TraceLeaf, a deletion request may concern:

- Personal information contained in support correspondence with TraceLeaf
- Information that TraceLeaf directly handles in connection with your use of the app
- Other personal data that TraceLeaf directly controls and is able to delete

TraceLeaf is designed as a local-first application. Your everyday diary entries are stored locally on your device and are not stored on TraceLeaf's own servers.

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your diary entries and other locally stored app data remain on your device unless you remove the relevant local data.

To remove locally stored TraceLeaf data, you can clear the app's storage or uninstall TraceLeaf from your device.

TraceLeaf does not maintain a server-side copy of your everyday diary entries that can be remotely deleted through a deletion request.

---

## Google Drive Backups

If you have enabled Google Drive Backup, your encrypted backup is stored in your Google Drive account.

TraceLeaf encrypts the backup on your device before it is uploaded.

You can disconnect Google Drive Backup through TraceLeaf where supported and manage your Google account's access to TraceLeaf through your Google account settings.

Disconnecting or revoking TraceLeaf's access does not necessarily delete an existing backup already stored by Google.

If you want to remove an existing Google Drive backup, you may need to use the account or storage controls provided by Google.

TraceLeaf does not represent that it can directly delete data stored in Google's systems.

If you submit a deletion request to TraceLeaf, we will process the portion of the request relating to personal data that TraceLeaf directly controls or can delete.

Google separately handles Google account information, Google Drive data, and related records according to Google's policies.

## Apple, Google Play, and RevenueCat Data

Purchases and purchase-related information may be handled by third-party services, including:

- Google Play
- Apple App Store
- RevenueCat

These services may retain purchase, transaction, entitlement, receipt, or related information according to their own privacy policies, contractual obligations, and legal requirements.

TraceLeaf does not control records maintained by these third-party services.

If you want to request deletion or exercise privacy rights concerning information held directly by one of these providers, you may need to contact the relevant provider or use its available privacy and account-management tools.

---

## Data That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained when required for legitimate purposes, such as:

- Purchase and transaction records maintained by Google Play or Apple App Store
- Purchase and entitlement information processed by RevenueCat
- Records required for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, security, or legal compliance
- Information that must be retained to comply with applicable laws or legal obligations
- Support correspondence that must be retained for legitimate business, dispute-resolution, or legal purposes

Third-party services may retain certain information according to their own policies and applicable legal requirements.

---

## Processing Your Request

We will review valid deletion requests and take appropriate action within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal, technical, and verification requirements.

If some data cannot be deleted because it is stored or controlled by a third party, or because it is required to be retained by law or for another legitimate purpose, we will retain only what is necessary for that purpose where applicable.

If we need additional information to verify your identity or request, we may contact you before processing the request.
