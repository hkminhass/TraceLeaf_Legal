# Privacy Policy — TraceLeaf

**Effective date:** August 30, 2026

**Contact:** traceleafofficial@gmail.com

## Introduction

TraceLeaf ("TraceLeaf", "we", "us", or "our") is a personal five-year diary application designed to help you record one line about your life each day.

We believe your memories should remain yours. TraceLeaf is designed as a local-first application: your diary entries are stored on your device by default, and TraceLeaf does not operate a server that stores your diary entries for everyday writing and reading.

This Privacy Policy explains what information TraceLeaf handles, where that information is stored, and what happens when you choose to use optional services such as purchases or cloud backup.

## Privacy at a Glance

- Your diary is stored on your device by default.
- You do not need to create an account with TraceLeaf to use the diary.
- We do not store your diary entries on our own servers.
- We do not sell your diary entries or personal information.
- We do not use your diary entries for advertising or behavioral profiling.
- TraceLeaf does not display advertisements.
- Purchases are processed through Google Play or Apple App Store.
- RevenueCat is used to validate purchases and manage purchase entitlements.
- Cloud backup is optional.
- When cloud backup is enabled, your diary backup is encrypted on your device before being uploaded to your selected cloud service.
- You can use TraceLeaf without enabling cloud backup.

## Information Stored on Your Device

TraceLeaf stores information locally on your device to provide the app's functionality.

This may include:

- Diary entries and memories
- Diary volume information and dates
- Favorite entries
- Search-related information
- Cover, font, and appearance preferences
- Reminder settings
- App lock preferences
- Local trial and purchase access information
- Other app settings and metadata required for the app to function

Your diary entries are not routinely transmitted to or stored on TraceLeaf's own servers.

If you uninstall TraceLeaf or clear its application data, information stored only on your device may be removed. You should export or back up information you wish to preserve before uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

## Diary Information We Do Not Collect

TraceLeaf does not send your diary entries to our own servers for:

- Advertising
- Behavioral profiling
- Selling or sharing with advertisers
- Training advertising profiles
- Routine analytics of the content of your diary

We do not operate a server containing your everyday diary entries.

If you voluntarily send diary content to us, for example by including it in an email to our support address, that information may be received and processed as part of providing support.

## Device Permissions

TraceLeaf may request certain device permissions depending on the features you choose to use.

### Notifications

TraceLeaf may request notification permission so that you can schedule daily writing reminders.

Notifications are optional and can be disabled through your device settings.

### Biometrics and Device Passcode

If you enable the app lock, TraceLeaf may use your device's supported biometric authentication or device passcode authentication to unlock the application.

TraceLeaf does not receive or store your fingerprint, face scan, biometric template, or device passcode.

Authentication is handled by the operating system and supported device security mechanisms.

## Purchases and RevenueCat

TraceLeaf offers optional one-time purchases for individual five-year diary volumes.

Purchases are processed through the applicable app store:

- Google Play on Android
- Apple App Store on iOS

TraceLeaf does not receive or store your payment card number, bank account information, or other payment credentials used by the app store.

TraceLeaf uses RevenueCat to help validate purchases, maintain purchase entitlements, and support the Restore Purchases functionality.

Depending on the platform and configuration, RevenueCat may receive information such as:

- An anonymous App User ID
- Device or platform information
- Purchase and transaction information
- Google Play purchase tokens or Apple purchase receipts
- Purchase and entitlement status

RevenueCat processes this information to provide its purchase management and transaction validation services.

Your diary entries are not sent to RevenueCat as part of normal purchase or restore operations.

RevenueCat's handling of information is also subject to its own privacy policy:

https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

## One-Time Purchases and Refunds

Each five-year volume is sold as a separate one-time purchase. Purchasing one volume does not automatically purchase, renew, or charge you for a future volume.

There are no recurring subscriptions for five-year volumes.

Purchases are generally final once completed. TraceLeaf does not promise refunds except where a refund is required by applicable law or provided through the applicable app store's refund process.

For purchases made through Google Play, refund requests may be submitted through Google Play's refund process. For purchases made through Apple, refund requests are handled through Apple's applicable refund process.

If you experience a problem where a purchase was completed but the purchased volume was not unlocked, you may contact us at:

traceleafofficial@gmail.com

We will assist with purchase and access issues where we can.

## Optional Cloud Backup

Cloud backup is an optional feature. TraceLeaf does not require you to enable cloud backup to write and read your diary on your device.

If you choose to enable cloud backup, your backup is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded to the cloud service you select.

TraceLeaf does not operate its own server for storing your diary backups.

### Google Drive

When Google Drive backup is enabled:

- You authenticate with Google through Google's authentication system.
- TraceLeaf requests access to the application-specific Google Drive storage area used for app data rather than general access to your Drive files.
- Your diary backup is encrypted on your device before upload.
- The encrypted backup is stored in your Google Drive account.
- TraceLeaf does not routinely receive or store an unencrypted copy of your diary on its own servers.

Google's handling of your Google account and Google Drive information is governed by Google's policies:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can disconnect Google Drive backup through the app where supported and manage or delete your backup through your Google account.

### iCloud

If iCloud backup is enabled in your version of TraceLeaf:

- Backups are stored using Apple's iCloud services associated with your Apple account.
- Your backup is encrypted by TraceLeaf on your device before upload.
- TraceLeaf does not operate its own server for storing your iCloud backup.

Apple's handling of iCloud and Apple account information is governed by Apple's policies:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

### Cloud Backup Security

TraceLeaf is designed so that encryption occurs on your device before a backup is uploaded.

However, no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Once a backup has been uploaded to Google Drive or iCloud, the relevant third party's infrastructure, security practices, and privacy policies also apply.

## PDF Export

The exported PDF is created on your device and may be saved or shared using your device's available file and sharing features.

TraceLeaf does not receive the exported PDF unless you voluntarily send it to us, such as by attaching it to a support email.
