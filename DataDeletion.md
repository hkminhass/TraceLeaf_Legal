# TraceLeaf — Data Deletion Request

## Requesting Deletion of Your Data

At TraceLeaf, we respect your privacy and provide users with a way to request deletion of their data.

To request deletion of your TraceLeaf data, please send an email to:

**traceleafofficial@gmail.com**

### What to Include

Please use the subject line:

**TraceLeaf Data Deletion Request**

In your email, please include enough information for us to identify your request. If you use Google Drive Backup, please also provide the Google account email address associated with your backup.

We may ask for additional information to verify the request and protect your data from unauthorized deletion.

---

## What Data Can Be Deleted?

Depending on how you use TraceLeaf, a deletion request may include:

- Information associated with your TraceLeaf use
- Your Google Drive backup data stored in your Google Drive
- Information associated with optional cloud backup functionality
- Other personal data that TraceLeaf is able to delete from its systems

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device

TraceLeaf is designed with local storage in mind. Your diary entries and other locally stored app data remain on your device unless you choose to delete them.

If you want to remove locally stored TraceLeaf data, you can delete the app and/or clear its app data from your device.

---

## Data That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained when required for legitimate purposes, such as:

- Google Play purchase and transaction records
- Records required for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, security, or legal compliance
- Information that must be retained to comply with applicable laws or legal obligations

Third-party services such as Google Play and RevenueCat may retain certain information according to their own policies and legal requirements.

---

## Google Drive Backups

If you have enabled Google Drive Backup, your backup is stored in your own Google Drive account.

You can also remove the backup directly from your Google Drive.

If you request deletion from TraceLeaf, we will process the deletion request for data that TraceLeaf controls or can delete.

---

## Processing Your Request

We will review valid deletion requests and take appropriate action within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal and technical requirements.

If some data cannot be deleted because it is required to be retained by law or by a third-party service, we will retain only what is necessary for that purpose.
